gantt

A Gantt chart, with no dependencies

Tasks sit in a tree next to a timeline you can drag. Move a task or stretch it, pull its progress along, or draw a link from one task to the next, and everything that waits on it moves too. Turn on the critical path to see which tasks decide the end date, and undo anything. Free and MIT licensed, for work that usually comes with a per-developer licence.
